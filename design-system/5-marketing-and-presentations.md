# Marketing and presentations

**Marketing is where the brand gets loud: the red block, the extruded edge and heavy Archivo type.** Product screenshots always sit on bone or ink, never on photos.

## Art direction

Two styles from the banner-design catalogue, nothing else:

1. **Flat / solid colour** — a full `mira-red` field, bone Archivo headline, one product screenshot on a `radius-xl` card with `shadow-extrude`.
2. **Data / infographic** — a big number as the hero (e.g. "82% answered in 20 s") on ink or bone, with the voice-waveform bars from the cover as the only ornament.

Avoid stock photos of smiling headset agents, glassmorphism, gradients and neon.

## Slides (sales decks and demos)

- 16:9, `surface` or `ink` backgrounds; title slide and section dividers are full `mira-red` with the extruded block.
- Archivo for titles (`display-l`), IBM Plex Sans for body (`lead` / `body-l`), numbers as heroes in Archivo.
- One idea per slide; a metrics slide shows at most 4 KPI tiles.
- Suggested demo deck (9 slides): problem (missed calls, blind managers) → Mira in one line → live call inside ERPNext → IVR builder → wallboard → Arabic AI summary → cloud or on-premise → pricing tiers → book a pilot.
- Charts follow the `chart-*` order; series 1 is red.

## Copy formulas

Lead with the customer's problem, then Mira's answer, then proof: "Every missed call is a lost order. Mira answers, routes and logs every call inside ERPNext — on our cloud or your servers."
